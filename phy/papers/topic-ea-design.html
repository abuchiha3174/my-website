Robust Multi-Mode Superconducting Circuit Optimized for Quantum Information Processing

P. García-Azorín, F. A. Cárdenas-López, ... M. Sanz — UPV/EHU Bilbao et al., arXiv:2407.18895 (2026)

Proposes the "Difluxmon," a multi-mode qubit found via evolutionary-algorithm search over circuit parameters, whose extra degrees of freedom escape the single-mode tradeoff between charge-noise and flux-noise resilience. Achieves a T2/gate-time ratio ~10× Transmon and ~2× Fluxonium, while staying robust to fabrication variation — a different lever on coherence (device design) than the pulse-suppression work in the other topic.
